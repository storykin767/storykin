# Facebook group posts

## Rules before posting anything

1. **Check each group's pinned rules for self-promotion.** Many gift and
   parenting groups ban it outright. Posting anyway gets you removed and often
   permanently banned, which costs more than the post is worth. If promotion is
   banned, ask an admin first — some allow it with permission.
2. **Disclose that you made it.** Posting as though you were a happy customer
   is against most group rules and is dishonest. It also performs worse: people
   respond to a person who built something.
3. **No link in the post body.** Facebook suppresses reach on posts with
   external links. Put the link in the **first comment**, immediately after
   posting.
4. **Attach a real illustration** from a generated book. Use the pins in
   `marketing/pins/` or a screenshot of a real preview page. The image is what
   stops the scroll.
5. **One group per day, maximum.** Posting to several at once triggers spam
   detection.
6. **Reply to every comment within the hour.** Engagement in the first hour
   decides how far the post travels.

### Note on the two groups already posted to

Those posts went up in April 2026, when book generation was broken — anyone who
clicked through would have filled in the form and watched it fail. Those
audiences saw the worst possible version of the product.

Do **not** immediately repost the same pitch. Instead, wait a few weeks and
post something genuinely new rather than a repeat: a photograph of a printed
book once the physical proof arrives, which is a different post rather than
the same one again. Reposting the same thing to a group that has already seen
it is the fastest way to get removed.

"Grandparents raising grandchildren" is worth returning to — it is the closest
match to the primary buyer of any group on the list.

## Follow-up

If a post gets traction, the highest-value reply is a **photograph of a real
printed book**, not more text. That is blocked until the first physical proof
is ordered — another reason to order it.

Do not post all three in one day. One group per day, and reply to everything.
