# Pinterest playbook

Pinterest is a **search engine**, not a social feed. Pins surface for months or
years after posting, and the text fields are what make that happen. Write for
someone typing a query, not for followers.

Account: Storykin (business). Domain claimed 23 August 2026 via the
`p:domain_verify` meta tag in `frontend/app/layout.tsx`.

## Cadence

New accounts get throttled for dumping. **One to two pins a day**, not five at
once. Spread these over the first week, then keep going — Pinterest rewards
consistency far more than volume.

After the first week, repin each image to a second relevant board a few weeks
apart. The same image on two boards with different descriptions reaches two
different searches.

## Seasonal timing

Pinterest planning behaviour runs **45-60 days ahead** of the event. That
means:

- **Christmas pins from late September**, peaking through October and November.
  Pinning Christmas content in December is too late.
- Birthday and grandparent pins are evergreen — post any time.
- Mother's Day content from mid-March.

## What to make next

The single best-performing Pinterest format for this product would be a
**real photograph of a printed book in a child's hands**. Nothing converts like
proof the physical object exists. That is blocked until the first physical
proof is ordered.
